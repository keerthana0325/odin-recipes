Oreo Milk Shake

Description

Oreo Milk shake is a sweet and tasty food item. It can be taken as chilled milkshake too.

Easy to preapre taking only minutes and serving the taste it is.

Ingredients

  • 6 chocolate sandwich cookies (such as Oreo)
  • 3 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 3/4 cup milk

Steps

  1. Blend chocolate sandwich cookies and milk.
  2. Then add a scoop of vanillaice cream and beat it.
  3. Pour into 2 glasses.
  4. Serve by sprinkling choco chips and ice cream.
