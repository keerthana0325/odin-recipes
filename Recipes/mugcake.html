Chocolate lava mug cake

Description

Choco lava mug cake is an easy go to recipe with less cooking time . A tasty and delightful dessert for when craving sweet.

A recipe that can me made with a very few ingredients......... so lets get started!!

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp all purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp of white sugar powder
  • 1 tbsp of cocoa powder
  • dark chocolate or choco chips
  • baking soda
  • milk
  • salt

Steps

  1. Take a cermaic Mug
  2. Add the flour, sugar powder, cocoa powder.
  3. Add a pinch of salt and baking soda.
  4. Mix all the dry ingredient well.
  5. Add milk spoon by spoon till it becomes a medium flowy texture.
  6. Microwave it for 2 minutes after pre heating.
  7. Ready to serve.
